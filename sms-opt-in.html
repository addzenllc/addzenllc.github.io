SMS Opt-In: Verbal Consent Script

Use: internal testing only. Messages go only to the mobile phones of AddZen LLC team members who agree to receive them. No messages go to clients or the public, and none are marketing.

Script read to each team member before their number is added:

"AddZen LLC will send test text messages to your mobile number to check that our test system delivers them. You'll get about 10 messages a month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Do you agree to receive these test messages?"

The number is added only after a spoken "yes". We record the date of consent and the number.
