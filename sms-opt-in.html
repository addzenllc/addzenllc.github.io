SMS Opt-In

Use: internal testing only. Messages go only to mobile numbers owned by the owner of AddZen LLC, the business's sole member. No messages go to clients or the public, and none are marketing.

Verbal consent script. Before any mobile number is added to the test list, its owner is read the following and must answer "yes":

"AddZen LLC will send automated test text messages to this mobile number, such as appointment confirmations and status updates, to check that the software we develop delivers them. You will receive about 10 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, or reply STOP at any time to opt out. Our SMS Terms are at https://addzenllc.github.io/sms-terms.html and our Privacy Policy is at https://addzenllc.github.io/privacy.html. Do you agree to receive these test messages? Please answer yes or no."

The number is added only after a spoken "yes". We record each number and the date of consent.

Confirmation of enrollment. The first message sent to a newly added number is:

AddZen LLC: You are enrolled in test text messages, about 10 per month. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

See our SMS Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
