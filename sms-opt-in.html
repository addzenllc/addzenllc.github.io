SMS Opt-In

Use: internal testing only. Messages go only to mobile numbers owned by the owner of AddZen LLC, the business's sole member. No messages go to clients or the public, and none are marketing.

How consent is given: the owner adds their own mobile numbers to the test list and, by doing so, agrees to the following:

"I agree to receive test text messages from AddZen LLC at this mobile number to check that our test system delivers them. About 10 messages a month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help."

No other numbers are added. We record each number and the date of consent.
